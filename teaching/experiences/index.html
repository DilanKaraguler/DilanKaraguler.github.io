Mentorship and leadership in teaching

Lead Teaching Assistant, Department of Mathematics, Michigan State University Aug 2024 – present

  • 2026 – 2027: co-leading a yearlong workshop series for first-year graduate TAs with Eloy Moreno-Nadales. Sessions so far:
    • Introductions: a gallery walk at whiteboards arranged in a circle, a conversation on what makes math instruction welcoming or alienating, and a “Math PhD passport” and department scavenger hunt that connect new students with senior graduate students.
    • Three Aspects of Teaching: group work and a gallery walk on (1) clarity and delivery (board layout, pacing, time traps), (2) communication and language (avoiding words like “trivial” or “obviously”, questioning and wait time) and (3) engagement and cohort needs, drawn from the TAs’ own classroom observations, to prepare their mock teaching.
